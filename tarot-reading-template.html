Tarot Reading

Past Attraction, Present Energy, and the Choice Ahead

Prepared for: [Name]
Date: [Date]
Question: Was there lust after his sister-in-law in the past, and is it still current? What is the real energy around this?
Cards: The Devil · Judgment · The Hanged Man · Five of Cups · Clarifier: The Lovers · Additional clarifier: The Moon
Important note: Tarot is symbolic and reflective. It cannot confirm a person’s private thoughts as objective fact, and this reading should not be used as proof, accusation, or surveillance. Use it as a tool for reflection and conversation—not as a verdict about someone’s character.

Overall Energy

This reading leans toward past temptation or obsession rather than clear, current, active pursuit. The Devil and Judgment suggest that something taboo or boundary-related was present, confronted, or brought into accountability. The Hanged Man and Five of Cups show a more suspended, regretful, or emotionally stuck present—not active pursuit. The Lovers, as clarifier, shifts the focus to values, choice, and integrity. The Moon adds ambiguity: there may still be hidden or subconscious emotional charge, but it is murky, private, and not clearly acted upon.

Card-by-Card

The Devil

This points to temptation, obsession, taboo attraction, or an unhealthy attachment. In the context of a sister-in-law, it suggests a boundary issue or forbidden desire—more about inner pull than necessarily outward action.

Judgment

Judgment brings reckoning, accountability, exposure, or an ending. It suggests the situation was judged, confronted, or became a matter of conscience. It is not usually a card of ongoing pursuit; it is more about awakening and consequence.

The Hanged Man

Current energy is suspended, passive, or on hold. He may be letting go, seeing it differently, or choosing not to act. It is not a card of active desire or pursuit.

Five of Cups

This is regret, guilt, disappointment, and focus on what was lost or wrong. It suggests emotional residue more than active lust.

Clarifier: The Lovers

The real issue is choice and values. This card asks: what does integrity require? It points to commitment, boundaries, and aligning behavior with values—not secret ongoing lust.

Additional Clarifier: The Moon

The Moon adds hidden feelings, subconscious attraction, fantasy, anxiety, ambiguity, or secrecy. It means the situation is not fully clear or fully resolved. There may be lingering emotional undercurrents he does not fully understand or admit—but it does not prove current active lust.

Past or Current?

More past than present in terms of action. The Devil + Judgment suggest the temptation existed and was reckoned with. The Hanged Man + Five of Cups suggest current suspension and regret, not pursuit. The Moon says something may still linger subconsciously—fantasy, guilt, curiosity, or unresolved taboo energy—but it is hidden and ambiguous. The Lovers says the current theme is a values-based choice, not confirmed ongoing desire.

Verdict: The reading suggests the lust or attraction was likely more past than current. What remains now is more likely guilt, regret, unresolved emotional charge, or a private temptation he is choosing not to act on. The Moon leaves room for hidden or subconscious feelings, but the overall spread does not clearly show current active lust or pursuit.

Gentle Guidance

  • Treat this as a reflection tool, not as proof of someone’s private thoughts.
  • If this reading is being shared in a relationship context, focus on boundaries, honesty, and values—not accusation.
  • The Lovers asks for a conscious choice: integrity, commitment, and clear boundaries.
  • The Five of Cups and Hanged Man suggest that releasing regret and seeing the situation differently may be more important than relitigating the past.

This reading is intended for reflection and emotional clarity. It is not a factual investigation, psychological diagnosis, or substitute for direct communication.
